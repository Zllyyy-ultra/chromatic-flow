# V0.3: evolving light volumes

2026-11-06. The user requested evolving colours and particle effects instead of a fixed coloured cloth, and selected a visibly music-driven direction.

## Changes

The rectangular surface is replaced by deterministic independent particles. Three procedural volumes (ribbons, three-arm spiral, nebula) share persistent particle seeds and blend continuously. Background dust contributes depth. Four palettes blend continuously; local colour coordinates travel through the particles rather than remaining tied to screen X. Soft dots, star rays and rings provide distinct particle appearances.

Music drives displacement, expansion, shimmer and the pace of colour/morph evolution. All music contributions are multiplied by sensitivity; 0% removes them. Manual formation and palette choices converge exponentially to avoid jumps. Automatic formation weights remain normalized and bounded. Texture-free WebGL 1 rendering retains one draw call and existing resolution limits.
